# S.A. Catering Elevated

S.A. CATERING — PREMIUM LANDING PAGE

Act as a Senior UI/UX Designer, Front-End Developer, Conversion Specialist, SEO Specialist, and Motion Designer.

Design and develop a premium, responsive single-page landing website for:

S.A. CATERING SERVICE

Tagline:

Pure Veg. Full of Variety.

S.A. Catering provides premium vegetarian catering for weddings, engagements, receptions, family functions, religious functions, corporate events, birthdays, and other celebrations.

The objective of this website is simple:

Build trust → Show the food → Show social proof → Answer questions → Collect event requirements → Convert the visitor to WhatsApp.
